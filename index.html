01 / WHO WE ARE 关于我们

Many paths.
One community.

共筑校友社区网络，打造资源共享平台

Our UCalgary experience is a beginning.
What we build together comes next.

UCalgary Chinese Alumni Network (CAN) is an affinity community under the UCalgary Alumni Association. We bring Chinese and Chinese-speaking alumni together through professional growth, cultural connection, and meaningful engagement with UCalgary.

Our purpose is to build an inclusive community, a supportive alumni network, and a long-term ecosystem of shared resources.
